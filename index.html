Our Commitment

AJN Software Development LLC respects your privacy. Muslim Lifestyle is designed to provide Islamic prayer, Qur'an, Hadith, and lifestyle features while giving users control over relevant device permissions.

1. Introduction

Muslim Lifestyle is an Islamic prayer and lifestyle application developed and operated by AJN Software Development LLC, a Missouri limited liability company.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is used, stored, and potentially transmitted when you use Muslim Lifestyle.

Some features operate locally on your device. Other features require connections to online services.

2. Information Used by the Application

Depending on the features you use, Muslim Lifestyle processes the following categories of information:

  • Location information: Device coordinates or a manually selected location used for prayer times, Qibla direction, and supported location-based features.
  • Prayer preferences: Prayer calculation settings, location selections, notification settings, and related preferences.
  • Qur'an preferences and progress: Bookmarks, reading progress, Khatam progress, and supported recitation settings.
  • Dhikr and lifestyle preferences: Dhikr counts, Ramadan-related settings, and other locally saved application preferences.
  • Optional profile information: Information that you choose to enter into supported application profile fields.
  • Network connection information: Technical information, including IP addresses, that may be visible to services receiving application requests.

3. Location Information

Muslim Lifestyle supports automatic GPS location and manual location selection.

When you choose automatic location, the application may request access to your device's approximate or precise location.

Location coordinates are used to determine local prayer times, calculate Qibla direction, and support other location-related functionality.

The application can save your selected prayer location on your device so it can be reused.

Muslim Lifestyle also uses Android location and geocoding functionality to obtain a readable location name. Depending on the device and its geocoding provider, this process may involve communication with an external service.

Certain optional location-based searches may transmit latitude and longitude to a configured service provider when those features are available and used.

You can deny or change location permissions through your device settings. You can also select a prayer location manually.

4. Prayer Times and Adhan Notifications

Muslim Lifestyle provides prayer-time calculations, prayer reminders, and Adhan audio playback.

The application may use Android notification permissions, scheduled alarms, wake locks, and background media services to deliver reminders and play Adhan audio.

These features may operate while the application is closed or the device is locked, depending on operating-system permissions and restrictions.

Prayer and Adhan preferences may be saved locally on your device.

5. Qur'an Features

Muslim Lifestyle includes locally available Qur'an content and provides additional Qur'an-related features.

Some Qur'an functions connect to an online application service to retrieve reciter information and optional audio resources.

These requests may include the selected Surah, verse, page, or recitation identifier.

The receiving service may also receive technical connection information, such as your device's public IP address.

Qur'an bookmarks, reading preferences, and supported progress information may be stored locally on your device.

6. Hadith Features

Muslim Lifestyle provides Hadith-related reading functionality.

Certain Hadith content is retrieved through an external content delivery service, including jsDelivr, using resources from the fawazahmed0 Hadith API project.

Hadith requests may identify the selected collection, language edition, and section.

The content delivery provider may process technical connection information, including IP addresses, under its own applicable privacy practices.

7. Nearby Mosques and Halal Places

Muslim Lifestyle includes functionality intended to support nearby mosque and halal-place searches.

These features require a configured online places service.

If a supported search service is configured and you use the feature, the application may send latitude and longitude to that service to retrieve nearby results.

When the required provider is not configured, these searches may be unavailable.

8. Information Stored Locally

Muslim Lifestyle uses device storage to remember supported application settings and user preferences.

Locally stored information may include:

  • Saved prayer locations.
  • Prayer calculation preferences.
  • Notification and Adhan settings.
  • Qur'an bookmarks and reading progress.
  • Khatam progress.
  • Dhikr counts.
  • Ramadan-related preferences.
  • Optional profile information.
  • Other application settings.

Locally stored information generally remains until you change it, clear application data, or uninstall the application, subject to device backup and restoration behavior.

9. Third-Party Services

Muslim Lifestyle uses or may interact with the following categories of services:

  • Android location and geocoding services: Used for device location and readable location information.
  • Muslim Lifestyle Qur'an service: Used for supported online Qur'an content and audio-related requests.
  • jsDelivr and the fawazahmed0 Hadith API: Used to retrieve supported Hadith content.
  • Optional places services: Used for mosque and halal searches when configured.

External services may process technical connection information necessary to receive and respond to requests.

Their handling of information may be governed by their own privacy policies.

10. How Information Is Used

Information processed by Muslim Lifestyle is used to support requested application functionality, including:

  • Calculating prayer times.
  • Determining Qibla direction.
  • Providing Adhan notifications.
  • Remembering preferences.
  • Displaying Qur'an and Hadith content.
  • Retrieving supported online audio.
  • Supporting optional nearby searches.
  • Maintaining application functionality.

11. Information Sharing

Muslim Lifestyle does not require users to publish their prayer preferences or reading progress publicly.

When you use network-connected features, information required for the request is transmitted to the relevant service.

Such requests may expose technical connection information, including IP addresses, to receiving providers.

Location coordinates may also be transmitted when a supported location-based service requires them.

No application, electronic storage system, or network transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.
