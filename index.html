Our Commitment: Muslim Lifestyle respects user privacy and aims to provide transparency about how information is handled when using the application.

1. Introduction

Muslim Lifestyle is an Islamic prayer and lifestyle application operated by Revive & Renew LLC, operating under the AJN Software Development name.

This Privacy Policy explains the categories of information that may be processed through Muslim Lifestyle, the purposes of processing, and the choices available to users.

2. Information Processed by the Application

Depending on the features you use and permissions you grant, Muslim Lifestyle may process:

  • Location information: Geographic information used to determine local prayer times.
  • Prayer preferences: Prayer calculation methods, notification settings, and related preferences.
  • Adhan preferences: Audio selections and playback settings.
  • Device information: Information required for application functionality, notifications, and compatibility.
  • Third-party service information: Information processed when the application communicates with external services.

3. Location Information

Muslim Lifestyle may request permission to access your device's location to determine prayer times appropriate for your geographic area.

You can allow or deny location permission through your device settings. Certain prayer-time features may be limited if location permission is unavailable.

Location information is used for prayer-related functionality. Depending on the services used by the application, location-related information may be processed locally or transmitted to an external provider.

4. Prayer Times and Adhan Notifications

Muslim Lifestyle provides prayer-time information, prayer reminders, and Adhan audio playback.

The application may use notification permissions, scheduled alarms, and background services to deliver reminders and play Adhan audio at prayer times.

These features may continue working while the application is closed or the device is locked, subject to operating system permissions and device restrictions.

5. Application Preferences and Local Storage

Muslim Lifestyle may store application preferences on your device, including prayer calculation settings, notification preferences, and Adhan audio choices.

Locally stored preferences may remain available until you change them, clear the application's storage, or uninstall the application.

6. Third-Party Services

Muslim Lifestyle may integrate third-party services to provide certain application features.

Such services may include location-related functionality, prayer-time information, and technical services needed to operate the application.

External providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

The specific third-party providers used by the application should be identified in this policy once the application's integrations are verified.

7. How Information Is Used

Information processed through Muslim Lifestyle may be used to:

  • Calculate or retrieve prayer times.
  • Deliver prayer reminders and Adhan notifications.
  • Remember application settings and preferences.
  • Support application functionality.
  • Maintain compatibility with supported devices.
  • Diagnose technical issues where applicable.

8. Information Sharing

Muslim Lifestyle does not require users to publicly share their prayer preferences.

Information may be processed by third-party providers when necessary for application functionality.

Any sharing or processing of personal information must be consistent with applicable privacy requirements and the services actually implemented in the application.

9. Data Security

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Preferences stored locally on your device may remain until you modify them, clear application data, or uninstall Muslim Lifestyle.

You may contact us to ask about personal information under our control or to request deletion where applicable.
